# Lessons Learned

Mistakes made before, and what to do instead, that apply beyond a single project.

## Belongs here

- A "learned my lesson" mistake: one that cost real time or caused a real problem, with a
  concrete example and what to do instead.
- Any lesson that can be stated without naming anything specific to one project. Lessons
  are general by default.
- One lesson per file, named for its subject in kebab-case.

## Doesn't belong here

- A lesson that only makes sense with one project's specifics → that project's
  `lessons-learned/`.
- A rule you now apply to every change → `principles/` (lessons get promoted there).
- A gap in a procedure: a skill should have caught or asked about it → fix the skill in
  the toolkit.
- A small one-off slip → nowhere.

## Finding a lesson

Scan the `summary` and `read-when` lines of the notes here; there's no index to maintain.
See the lessons section of `how-this-brain-works/README.md` for the format and lifecycle.
